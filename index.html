About Me

From the age of 15, I became serious about fitness. Over time, I learned a lot about my body, training and mindset. I have been involved in sport throughout my life, from grassroots football to the semi-professional game. Studying the body properly helped me understand what it actually means to train well, balancing performance with recovery and keeping your body feeling good long term.
